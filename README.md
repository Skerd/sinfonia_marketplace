# marketplace Module (Sinfonia)

Panel pages of the Arpeggio services marketplace (listings, task requests, bids, services orders and their milestones, deliveries, revisions, bookings, reviews, disputes, provider profiles) and its public portal section `services` (service and provider pages, the buyer's service orders and jobs).

Contracts live in armonia at `armonia/src/modules/marketplace/`; the runtime in maestro at `maestro/modules/marketplace/`. Depends on `core` and `finance` only (D142).

## Status

Being moved out of eCommerce (roadmap P11, D142). Until P11.4 and P11.5 land, the pages still live in `sinfonia/src/modules/eCommerce`.
